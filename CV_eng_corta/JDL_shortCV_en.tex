\section{About me}\label{about-me}

\begin{minipage}[c]{0.9\linewidth}
  \begin{footnotesize}
    I am a Full Professor and researcher at \href{https://jdleongomez.info/es/team/}{\textit{\textbf{EvoCo}: Human Behaviour and Evolution Lab}}, and leader of the \href{https://grupo-codec.netlify.app/}{\textit{\textbf{CODEC}: Cognitive and Behavioural Sciences Research Group}} (classified as \href{https://scienti.minciencias.gov.co/gruplac/jsp/visualiza/visualizagr.jsp?nro=00000000001446}{\textbf{A1}}), within the \href{https://www.uelbosque.edu.co/psicologia}{Faculty of Psychology} at the \href{https://www.uelbosque.edu.co/}{Universidad El Bosque} in Bogotá, Colombia. I am also a founding and board member of the \textbf{\textit{Latin American Association for Human Behavioral and Evolutionary Sciences}} (\textbf{\textit{LAAHBES}}). \par \vspace{0.05em}
    \textbf{Research:} My work focuses on human vocal communication, mate choice, musicality, and hormonal influences on behaviour. I have published pioneering studies on vocal modulation in social and courtship contexts.\par \vspace{0.05em}
    \textbf{Open Science:} I am interested in quantitative methods and programming, especially in \textbf{R}, as tools to promote open and reproducible science. In line with these values, I serve as \textit{Data Editor} at \href{https://royalsocietypublishing.org/journal/rspb}{Proceedings of the Royal Society B}, \textit{Associate Editor}  at \href{https://evohuman.org}{Evolutionary Dynamics of Human Behavior (EDHB)}, and as \href{https://rr.peercommunityin.org/about/recommenders}{recommender} (associate editor) at \href{https://rr.peercommunityin.org/}{PCI Registered Reports}. I also direct \href{https://mc-ueb.netlify.app/}{\textbf{MetaCiencia}}, a research incubator on reproducibility and open science.
  \end{footnotesize}
